First Ever Table Top Test

This video shows SeaStar’s first ever tabletop test. During this test, we verified that the software architecture was communicating correctly and that there was stable communication between the topside control system and the ROV.

First Power Supply Test

This vlog shows our first power supply test for the SeaStar ROV system. During this stage we checked that the electrical setup could safely provide power to the system before adding more subsystems.

Ocean Enclosure Leak Test

This vlog shows our ocean enclosure leak test. We tested the enclosure without internal electronics to safely validate the waterproof seal before placing hardware inside. After submerging it in the ocean, no water entered the enclosure, confirming that the sealing design worked successfully!
