Mission

SeaStar focuses on two core tasks: (1) collecting algae samples safely and repeatably, and (2) measuring dissolved oxygen levels to help evaluate water quality.

Problem

Manual sampling can be slow, inconsistent, and difficult in shallow, murky, or hard-to-reach areas.

Approach

A small ROV with a simple sampling end-effector, real-time DO sensing, and onboard logging for repeatable runs.

Deliverables

  • Operational ROV platform
  • Algae sample collection method
  • DO readings + timestamped log
  • Photo/video documentation

Validation

  • Sensor calibration + repeatability check
  • Sampling success rate over N trials
  • Test plan + results (table/plots)
