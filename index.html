About Algae Blooms

Algae blooms occur when algae grow out of control. While some blooms are harmless, others can produce toxins that harm the environment, animals, and humans.

GREEN ALGAE

Common in freshwater, important for oxygen production.

RED ALGAE

Mostly marine, used in food products.

BROWN ALGAE

Includes kelp, forms underwater forests.
